How to play
  1. Both fleets are placed at random. Ships are straight and never touch, not even at the corners.
  2. You fire first. Each side fires one shot per turn, even after a hit.
  3. Fire at the enemy board. Your own fleet and the computer's shots are on the second board.
  4. Firing at a square twice does not cost your turn.
  5. The first side to sink every enemy ship wins.
